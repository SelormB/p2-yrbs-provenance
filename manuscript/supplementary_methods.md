# Supplementary methods (draft)

DRAFT — UNVERIFIED. Assembled from the protocol. Tables marked S are generated by the pipeline.

**S1. Provenance taxonomy.** See `docs/taxonomy.md`, sections 1–3. That page gives the conceptual sources, what the released data can identify, and the cell codes.

**S2. Sensitivity scenarios.** See `src/p2prov/scenarios.py` (13 scenarios × 2 outcomes = 26 tasks) and `docs/protocol.md` §11.

**S3. Comparator specifications.** See `docs/protocol.md` §7 and the docstrings in `src/p2prov/methods.py`.
- Logistic regression: L2 penalty, C = 1, lbfgs solver.
- Gradient boosting: scikit-learn `HistGradientBoostingClassifier`, learning rate 0.05, up to 500 iterations, 31 leaves, ≥50 samples per leaf, L2 = 1, early stopping on an internal 15% slice of the training data.
- Multiple imputation: `IterativeImputer` with BayesianRidge, posterior sampling, 5 iterations, 15 nearest features, m = 5. The imputation model is fit on a seeded training subsample of at most 200,000 rows.
- Pattern submodels: each availability pattern is fit on the training rows whose surveys asked at least that pattern's items. A pattern with fewer than 50 training events falls back to the submodel for items asked in every survey.

**S4. Design-based bootstrap.**
- In each test-set replicate, n_h PSUs are drawn with replacement within each stratum h. Strata are unique per site-year.
- Strata with a single PSU are kept unchanged.
- Each row's multiplicity multiplies its survey weight (weighted metrics) or a unit weight (unweighted metrics).
- Confidence intervals are percentile intervals.
- Paired contrasts use common draws.

**S5. Calibration.**
- Calibration slope: logistic regression of the outcome on logit(p).
- Calibration intercept: logistic regression with logit(p) as an offset.
- Both use weighted Newton-Raphson.

**S6. Thresholds.**
- Three rules, all applied on the validation set: the score at 80% weighted sensitivity, and the scores at the top 10% and top 5% of weighted predicted risk.
- Each threshold is then applied unchanged to the test set.

**S7. Software.** Python ≥3.10; numpy, pandas, scikit-learn, scipy, matplotlib (versions in `environment.yml`). Unit and integration tests are in `tests/`.
